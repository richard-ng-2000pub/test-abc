How to investigate

1. Compare BEFORE and AFTER. Every letter identifies a character. A1 is the upper-left square; columns move left to right, rows top to bottom.

2. Respect the building. No diagonal moves, no furniture crossings, and room walls can only be crossed at marked doors. The outside door does not permit leaving the map.

3. Use the common clock. Living characters move one orthogonal square per step on a valid shortest route. The victim stops moving at the crime scene after the murder.

4. Deduce killer + death step. A suspect must reach the victim at the right moment and still finish at their observed AFTER position. A nearby suspect may be innocent.

5. Check your theory. Use the notebook and accusation fields. Open Reconstruction only when you want to inspect a stored valid timeline.

The stored reconstruction is one legal witness timeline. The murderer/time pair is uniquely checked, but individual routes can have alternatives.
